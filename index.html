Saucy Steve the Skateboarding Sloth

Score: 0

Tickets: 0

State: Hub

Ride: Skateboard

Combo: None

Skate the city and rack up points to earn tickets.
Controls
  • Move: WASD or Arrow keys (supports 8-direction)
  • Push for speed: K
  • Ollie/Jump: J
  • Spin: L (queue 180-degree increments during the airborne window; score is awarded on clean landing)
  • Brake in the hub: Space (while grounded); S/Down steer south
  • Switch ride: Z (previous), X (next)
  • Enter competition zone: E
  • Return to hub (in competition): R
  • Controller (PS5/USB): Left stick steer, R2 push, X jump, Square spin
  • Controller extras: Circle/B enter event, Options return to hub, L1/R1 switch ride
Milestone 1 mechanics
  • Hub movement has momentum, 8-direction steering, camera follow, and world boundaries
  • Ramps provide lift; parked cars and buildings are solid obstacles
  • Rails are visual landmarks; contact-based grinding is deferred
  • Queue Spin during the airborne window; spin points are awarded only after a clean landing
  • Complete the practice line objective: Ollie, Spin, then Land
  • Score earns 1 ticket per 300 total points
  • Switch between Skateboard, Razor Scooter, Roller Blades, Quad Rollerskates, and BMX Bike
  • Spend 1 ticket to enter Halfpipe, Downhill, Slalom, or Jump competitions
  • Competition results award medals by score; first place awards 1 ticket
  • Landing on an obstacle or missing spin alignment causes a no-score bail and brief recovery
